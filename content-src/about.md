Hi, I’m Mark Schultz-Wu. I work on **fully homomorphic encryption (FHE)** at
[Fabric Cryptography](https://www.fabriccryptography.com/), building hardware
and software for cryptographic computation.

Before industry I did a PhD at UC San Diego, where most of my work was in
**lattice cryptography**. This blog is a place to store thoughts on lattices,
FHE, and assorted theoretical computer science.
